Solana copytrade tracker
Find who deployed a token
Pulls the token's creation transaction and reads who paid for it — usually the deployer's wallet. Less reliable for tokens launched through a program/factory.
Trace a wallet's funding lineage
Follows who a known wallet funded, who those wallets funded, and so on — checking every wallet along the way for a buy of the target token. Only catches on-chain funding — won't find a burner funded straight from a CEX withdrawal.
Cross-check two tokens
Find token A's whales, then check which of them also bought token B. Nothing gets saved — one-shot lookup.
